Pond & Creek Bank Cleanup

Clear overgrown brush, vines, and saplings from accessible pond edges and creek banks in New Market and across North Alabama. We can open up access without clearing every tree along the bank.

Get a Free Quote Call (256) 513-9076

Clear the Overgrowth, Keep the Trees You Want

Whether you want to get back to the edge of your pond or get through a stretch of brush along a creek, we can focus on the areas you want opened up. During the site visit, we’ll walk the accessible sections with you and discuss which trees and vegetation to leave in place.

What Bank Cleanup Includes

We clear brush, vines, briars, and suitable saplings along accessible sections of the bank. We look at the slope, ground conditions, and equipment access before recommending brush clearing or forestry mulching. This service is for vegetation on land, not dredging, work in the water, or bank reconstruction.

What to Send for a Quote

Send the property location, photos of the bank and equipment access, and the approximate length you want cleared. Let us know whether you want a walking path, a few access points, or a longer stretch opened up. We’re based in New Market, Alabama, and typically work within about 50 miles.

Get a Free On-Site Estimate

Tell us what’s overgrown and what you want to keep. Photos of the bank and how to reach it help us assess the job.
