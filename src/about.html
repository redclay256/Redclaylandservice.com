Our Story

Red Clay Land Service was founded right here in North Alabama by a military veteran who traded one kind of discipline for another — running a land clearing company the same way: show up, do the job right, and stand behind the work.

We started with forestry mulching and brush clearing for local landowners, and have since grown to cover fire mitigation and right-of-way work across Madison County and the surrounding North Alabama communities. What hasn't changed is the approach: heavy-duty equipment, experienced operators, and straightforward communication from the first phone call to the final walkthrough.

Our Mission

Give North Alabama property owners a faster, cleaner alternative to traditional land clearing — one that protects the soil, skips the burn piles, and gets land back into usable shape without the wait.

What We Stand For

  • Veteran-owned and operated built on discipline, reliability, and getting the job done right, every time
  • Straight answers we tell you what your land actually needs — no upselling, no surprises
  • Respect for your property we treat every job site, big or small, like it's our own land
  • Reliable scheduling we show up when we say we will, and most jobs are completed within 1–2 weeks of booking
