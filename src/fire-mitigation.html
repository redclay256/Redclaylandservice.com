What Is Fire Mitigation?

Dry brush, dead undergrowth, and dense timber understory can turn a small spark into a serious wildfire risk — especially on rural properties surrounded by woods. Fire mitigation clears out the fuel that lets fires spread quickly, giving you and first responders a fighting chance if one ever starts.

We create defensible space around structures and cut firebreaks through wooded areas, thinning out the ladder fuel (low brush and small trees) that lets ground fires climb into the tree canopy.

What We Do

  • Defensible space around structures clear a buffer zone of 30–100 ft around homes, barns, and outbuildings
  • Firebreaks cut clear lanes through timber and brush to slow or stop fire spread
  • Understory thinning remove dense low brush and small trees that act as “ladder fuel”
  • Dead & downed debris removal clear dry fuel that accumulates after storms or seasonal dieback

Why Fire Mitigation Matters

  • Slower fire spread reduced fuel load means fires move slower and stay smaller
  • Protects structures defensible space gives homes and barns a real buffer from wildfire
  • Easier for first responders clear access and firebreaks make it safer to fight a fire if one starts
  • Peace of mind especially valuable for properties bordering timberland or national forest

Ideal For

  • Rural homeowners properties surrounded by woods or dense brush
  • Cabins & hunting camps seasonal properties that sit unattended for stretches of time
  • Insurance-related requests some insurers require defensible space around structures
  • Land bordering timber properties adjacent to national forest or large wooded tracts
