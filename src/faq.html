We quote forestry mulching by the job, not at a flat per-acre rate. Price depends on the area you want cleared, vegetation size and density, slope, equipment access, and how far we need to travel. Mobilization and turf protection, if needed, can also affect the quote. For a free on-site estimate, send the property location, approximate acreage, photos, and what you want to clear or keep.

Forestry mulching grinds brush and suitable trees into mulch that stays on the ground. It is often a good fit for selective clearing, access trails, and overgrown acreage. If you need roots and stumps removed, debris hauled off, or a site graded for construction, you may need a different method or additional work.

Most residential and small acreage jobs are completed within a few days once we're on-site, and we can usually get a crew scheduled within 1–2 weeks of booking. Larger multi-acre or commercial projects are scoped individually during your estimate.

We mulch dense brush, saplings, and suitable small trees. Diameter, species, slope, and equipment access affect what can be mulched. During the estimate, we'll flag larger trees that may need separate work and discuss any trees you want to keep.

With forestry mulching, debris is ground into mulch and left on-site — there's no hauling or burning required. If a project calls for debris removal instead (for example, larger felled timber), we'll discuss that as part of your quote.
