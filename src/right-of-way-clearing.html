What Is Right-of-Way Clearing?

Right-of-way clearing keeps utility corridors, access roads, and property line easements open and passable — whether that's a power line easement, a shared access road, or a boundary line that needs to be clearly re-established for a survey.

We work directly with property owners, contractors, and utility companies to clear and maintain these corridors efficiently, on schedule, and to the specifications required for the job.

What We Clear

  • Utility corridor clearing & maintenance keep power line and pipeline easements clear of encroaching vegetation
  • Access road establishment open up or widen roads for equipment, construction, or emergency access
  • Fence & survey line clearing re-establish clear, visible property boundaries
  • Culvert & drainage clearing remove brush and debris blocking drainage paths and culverts

Who We Work With

  • Utility companies & contractors reliable corridor clearing that meets project specs and timelines
  • Farmers & landowners opening or maintaining access across large tracts of land
  • Developers clearing access roads ahead of construction or subdivision work
  • Property owners resolving boundary disputes with a clearly cleared and visible line

Ideal For

  • Utility & pipeline corridors recurring or one-time vegetation management along easements
  • Agricultural access clearing lanes and roads across working farmland
  • Boundary re-establishment clearing survey lines so property boundaries are unmistakable
  • New development access opening roads ahead of building or subdivision projects
