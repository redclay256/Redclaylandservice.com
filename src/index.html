What We Do

Land Clearing Services

From opening up a fence line to reclaiming overgrown acreage, we recommend a clearing method based on your terrain, vegetation, and plans for the property.

Forestry Mulching

We grind brush, saplings, and suitable small trees into mulch that stays on the property. We check tree size, slope, and access before recommending mulching.

  • Selective clearing around trees you want to keep
  • Mulch left on the ground
  • Ask about larger trees and stump removal separately

Brush Clearing

Dense undergrowth, overgrown fence lines, and invasive vegetation removed efficiently. Ideal for pastures, homesites, and property lines.

Fire Mitigation

Reduce brush and other vegetation that can fuel a wildfire. We help create defensible space around structures and clear firebreaks where the site allows.

Right-of-Way Clearing

Utility corridors, road access, fence lines, and easements cleared and maintained. We work with property owners, contractors, and utility companies.

Pond & Creek Bank Cleanup

Cut back overgrown brush, vines, and saplings to regain access along pond edges and creek banks.
